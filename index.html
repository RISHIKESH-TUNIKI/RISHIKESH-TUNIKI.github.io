From the database schema to the last pixel of the UI, I like owning the whole thing and making it feel good to use.

I'm the Product Lead at AumTech and a full-stack .NET and Angular developer. I joined in August 2024 as a data analytics and Python trainee, became a software engineer three months later, an IT tech lead in February 2025, and product lead in April 2026.
